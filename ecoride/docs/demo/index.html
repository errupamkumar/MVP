Part 1 · video 1:57 · 17 steps

Rider app

A new rider signs up, books from the phone's GPS location with an upfront fare and a promo, follows the cab live, starts the trip with a PIN, and rates the driver from a receipt that checks the fare against the GPS distance.

Phone screen, 390 × 844. The middle of the drive to the drop plays 2.5× faster.

  1. R01

    Welcome

    The rider app opens on a welcome screen with sign-up and sign-in.

  2. R02

    Create an account

    Sign-up asks for a name, an Indian mobile number, an optional email and a password (8+ characters).

  3. R03

    Home: your location and cars nearby

    The phone's GPS sets the pickup and labels it with the street. Free cars around the rider are drawn on the map and refresh every 5 s. A new rider gets ₹200 in the wallet and the FIRST50 code.

  4. R04

    Where to?

    Search covers curated city places and OpenStreetMap addresses, with each result's distance from the pickup.

  5. R05

    Choose a ride: upfront fares

    The road route (OSRM, OpenStreetMap) sets distance and time; every product shows its upfront fare and the nearest free car's ETA.

  6. R06

    Promo applied

    The promo is checked on the server (validity, uses per rider, minimum fare) and the discounted fare replaces the price.

  7. R07

    Finding your ride

    Searching: the rider sees how many drivers have been asked; one driver at a time gets 15 s to accept.

  8. R08

    Driver on the way

    The rider sees the car move on the map with a live ETA and distance, the driver and plate, and the 4-digit PIN to start the trip.

  9. R09

    Live tracking: the car approaches

    Every GPS fix moves the car; distance left is measured along the road route, and the ETA counts down.

  10. R10

    Your driver has arrived

    Arrival is only accepted within 250 m of the pickup. Free waiting counts down; waiting charges start after it.

  11. R11

    On trip

    Remaining distance and arrival time update live; the GPS-measured distance builds up next to the planned route. SOS is one tap away.

  12. R12

    Receipt

    Total fare, paid by UPI, and the rating card.

  13. R13

    Rate and tip

    Stars, compliments and an optional tip that goes to the driver in full.

  14. R14

    Distance check

    The GPS distance driven with the rider on board against the planned road route: within 20%, so the upfront price stands. Beyond it, the fare is recalculated from the GPS distance.

  15. R15

    Fare breakdown

    Every line adds up to the total: base fare for the first 3 km, distance beyond it, time, the FIRST50 promo, 5% GST and rounding to whole rupees.

  16. R16

    Your rides

    Every ride with its date, route, fare and status; tap one for its receipt.

  17. R17

    Wallet

    The wallet balance with the ₹200 welcome credit, quick UPI top-ups and the transaction list.

Part 2 · video 1:55 · 15 steps

Driver app

A separate app with its own accounts. An approved driver goes online, takes the request inside its 15-second window, drives to the pickup and the drop, starts with the rider's PIN and sees the earnings. A new driver applies and goes online once an admin approves.

Phone screen, 390 × 844. On a laptop the app replays the drive along the road route at 15× real time; the middle of the drive to the drop plays 2.5× faster again.

  1. D01

    Driver app welcome

    The driver app is a separate app with its own sign-up (Apply to drive) and sign-in.

  2. D02

    Offline home

    Signed in and offline: today's earnings, trips, rating and acceptance rate, and one big Go online button.

  3. D03

    Online and waiting for requests

    Online: the cab's position goes to the server every second (simulated GPS in this laptop demo; real GPS on a phone).

  4. D04

    Ride request with countdown

    The request card: what the driver earns (80% of the fare), pickup distance and ETA, trip distance and time, payment method, rider rating, and a countdown ring.

  5. D05

    Navigating to the pickup

    Live distance and time to the pickup, measured along the road route; Navigate opens turn-by-turn directions; the rider's shared position shows too.

  6. D06

    Start with the rider's PIN

    The trip starts only with the 4-digit PIN from the rider's app; a wrong PIN is refused.

  7. D07

    Driving to the drop

    The driver sees the distance left, the GPS distance so far, the fare and their earning.

  8. D08

    Trip complete

    GPS distance vs the planned route, the fare and the driver's share. UPI was charged automatically; with cash the driver confirms "Cash collected".

  9. D09

    Earnings

    Today and the last 7 days, cash kept on cash rides, the Monday payout balance and the activity ledger (trip share, tip).

  10. D10

    Profile

    The driver's vehicle, licence, rating and the Demo GPS setting (simulated drive on a laptop, real GPS on a phone).

  11. D11

    Apply: about you

    Step 1 of 3: name as on the licence, mobile number, optional email, password.

  12. D12

    Apply: your vehicle

    Step 2: ride type, make, model, colour, year and registration number (checked for format and duplicates).

  13. D13

    Apply: licence and documents

    Step 3: driving licence number; documents are checked by an admin at approval.

  14. D14

    Application under review

    The applicant can sign in but cannot go online until an EcoRide admin approves the account.

  15. D15

    Approved driver goes online

    Once approved, the new driver goes online and starts receiving requests.

Part 3 · video 1:14 · 13 steps

Admin console

The web console has complete access: the dashboard, a live map of every driver and ride, the ride just taken with its GPS trace, driver approval, fares, surge, finance, settings and the audit log.

Desktop browser, 1600 × 900. The console joins while the ride is in progress, so the dashboard and the live map show the trip.

  1. A01

    Admin sign-in

    A separate operations account signs in to the web console.

  2. A02

    Dashboard

    Today's gross bookings and commission, rides right now, drivers online, pickup time, rating, plus 14-day trend, ride-type mix, hourly demand, latest rides, driver applications and SOS.

  3. A03

    Live map

    Refreshed every 3 s. Rahul's car is on Priya's trip; named cars are real drivers, the rest is the simulated fleet that keeps the demo city busy.

  4. A04

    Rides

    Every ride with filters by status, type and text search.

  5. A05

    Ride detail: planned route vs GPS trace

    The planned road route (grey) against the GPS trace to the pickup (green) and with the rider (blue), with planned, GPS and dead-mileage distances, the people and the fare quoted vs final.

  6. A06

    Ride timeline and dispatch

    Every event with its time (request, offer, accept, arrival, PIN start, fare check, payment, ratings) and each dispatch attempt with distance and answer time.

  7. A07

    Driver application

    The applicant's licence and vehicle, ready to approve or reject (with a reason).

  8. A08

    Driver approved

    Approved: the driver can go online at once. Suspend, take offline and pay out are on the same page.

  9. A09

    Fares

    Base fare covering the first 3 km, per km, per minute, minimum fare, free waiting, waiting charge and cancellation fee for each product.

  10. A10

    Surge zones

    A manual 1.3x surge set on the Hinjewadi IT Park zone; riders booking there now see the surged upfront fare.

  11. A11

    Finance

    Totals for the period, gross bookings per day, how riders paid, ride-type mix, driver payout balances and every payment.

  12. A12

    Settings

    Each rule with its bounds; changes apply at once and are audited. Reset demo data rebuilds the demo.

  13. A13

    Audit log

    Who approved the driver and changed the surge zone, and when.

For the team

Run it yourself

Start

  • API: cd ecoride/backend, mvn -DskipTests package, java -jar target/ecoride-api.jar (port 9080, MySQL root/root)
  • Apps: cd ecoride, npm install, then npm run rider, npm run driver (Expo: web or Expo Go) and npm run admin
  • Every API start reseeds the demo: 25 drivers, 38 riders, 30 days of rides

How it was checked

  • 35 backend tests on MySQL: fares, route tracking, the full ride lifecycle, dispatch cascade, cancellation and no-show fees, GPS jump filtering, role separation, races
  • 53 API checks of the whole journey (scripts/smoke.mjs)
  • This recording: three videos and 45 steps from one live run, no console errors

Market rules built in

  • Base fare covers the first 3 km; drivers keep 80% of the fare (MoRTH 2025)
  • Surge capped at 1.5× (Maharashtra)
  • 15 s to accept, free cancel window, waiting charge after free wait, 5% GST
