Journey 1 of 3 · phone app

The employee books and rides

A. Raghavan needs to get from Gate 2 to the Blast Furnace without phoning the transport desk. Only two steps need any typing.

Signed in asLHS-40218Cost centreCC-4471 · Operations
E1

Sign in

One sign-in screen for everyone. Employees use their P. No., drivers their driver ID, the desk a username. The demo accounts below the form fill both fields in one tap.

Do
Tap Rider · LHS-40218, then Sign in.
Behind the screen
A 12-hour session (one shift) is kept in the phone's secure store. Each role lands in its own app: rider, driver or desk.
E2

Home

A greeting, one big Where to? button, saved places built from the rider's own trips this month, the count of upcoming rides and the cost centre every ride is charged to.

Do
Tap Where to? Tapping a saved place instead rebooks that trip in one tap.
Behind the screen
Saved places are the stops the rider actually travels to. A live ride, when there is one, sits at the top of this screen.
E3

Choose where to go

With the pickup set to Gate 2, the list shows only stops a route actually reaches from there, each with its route, ride time and distance.

Do
Pick Blast Furnace (Route 1 · 15 min · 3.4 km).
Behind the screen
Stops and their order are master data. Cabs serve a route in sequence and never double back, so a stop behind the rider is never offered.
E4

Book a ride

From and To are set. When offers Now or a later pickup; Seats sets how many are travelling.

Do
Keep Now and 1 seat.
Behind the screen
Rides can be scheduled up to 7 days ahead and are matched 20 minutes before pickup. A group larger than the 3-rider cap is steered to an exclusive cab.
E5

Rider details

Name, contact number and email come from the HR record and stay editable. Booking for a guest lets the employee book for a visitor or colleague.

Do
Tap Show ride options.
Behind the screen
HR records go stale, so a wrong phone number never blocks a booking; it is the number the driver calls. A guest's ride is charged to the host's cost centre.
E6

Ride options

Three ways to ride on one screen. Shuttle S-03 runs to a timetable and needs no booking. Shared cab C-12 has 2 of 3 seats taken and is at Gate 2 now. Exclusive cab needs approval from the rider's HOD. The occupancy rule and the cost centre are shown before confirming.

Do
Keep Shared cab C-12 selected and tap Confirm ride.
Behind the screen
The matching engine checked every cab on duty against hard rules, then scored the rest on ETA, detour, empty kilometres and seats already filled. C-12 already carries M. Iyer and S. Banerjee, pooled the moment the driver signed on (D3).

"Pick-up runs carry three riders, maximum. A full cab is never offered a fourth."

E7

Booking confirmed

The moment the booking succeeds: the cab's ETA, progress (Booked · Driver assigned · On the way · At your stop · On trip), the vehicle number MH 12 AB 4412, cab C-12, driver S. Kumar with a Call button, and the promised pickup time.

Behind the screen
The screen refreshes every 4 seconds while the ride is live. A retried tap on Confirm never books twice: each attempt carries its own request key.

"Vehicle number and driver contact are the two things people phone the desk for today. That call is gone."

E8

Boarding OTP and tracking link

A 4-digit boarding OTP to show the driver, and a live tracking link with Share trip and Copy link.

Do
Optionally open the link on another screen (E9).
Behind the screen
The OTP proves the right rider boards the right cab, which is what makes shared rides auditable. The link can go to a manager or family for a night trip.
E9

The shared tracking link

What anyone with the link sees, with no login: route, vehicle number, cab, where it was last seen and the stops ahead. It refreshes every 5 seconds.

Behind the screen
The page shows the vehicle, never the people: no names, no phone numbers. It stops working when the trip closes (E14).

Meanwhile the driver taps Arrived at Gate 2 (D4).

E10

Your cab has arrived

The screen flips as soon as the driver arrives: large OTP digits under Show this to the driver, and the seat rule in plain words, "3 of 3 riders on this pick-up · seat 4 held free by policy".

Do
Show the code; the driver types it (D5–D6).
Behind the screen
The rider is also notified "Cab C-12 is at Gate 2", with the OTP and the 5-minute wait.
E11

Trip in progress

Once the driver accepts the OTP: the drop ETA (16 min to Blast Furnace), where the cab was last seen, and the vehicle and driver.

Behind the screen
The trip started when the first rider boarded. The OTP is no longer needed, so it is no longer shown.
E12

Stops on this run

Every stop on the run up to the rider's drop: Gate 2 (cab is here), Coke Plant (next stop), Blast Furnace (your drop). "Sharing with 2 colleagues · 3 of 3 seats used", and a red SOS button.

Behind the screen
SOS alerts the transport desk and plant security together, with the cab's live location.

Meanwhile the driver drops M. Iyer at Coke Plant, then arrives at Blast Furnace (D7–D8).

E13

Trip complete, charged and rated

Gate 2 to Blast Furnace · 3.4 km · Shared. Charged to CC-4471 · Operations: ₹22.86. Below it, a rating in one tap: stars plus tags such as On time and Clean cab.

Do
Tap five stars, On time, Clean cab, then Submit rating.
Behind the screen
Arriving at the drop closed the ride; in the plant a GPS geofence does this without a tap. The trip cost ₹61.20 (3.4 km × the cab's rate), split by kilometres ridden: 2.3 km for M. Iyer and 3.4 km for each of the other two, across three cost centres, to the paisa.

"Ratings feed the vendor SLA report, not just a score."

E14

The link expires

The same shared link now reads This trip has ended.

Behind the screen
Nobody can follow the vehicle once the rider has been dropped.
E15

My trips · Past

Every completed ride with its date, route, distance, cab, charge and rating. The ride just taken is at the top: 3.4 km · C-12 · ₹22.86 · 5★.

Behind the screen
This is what employees use to query a charge, and what finance uses to answer them.
E16

My trips · Upcoming

A ride booked ahead: tomorrow 08:05, Gate 2 to Admin Block, Scheduled. The Cancelled tab keeps cancellations and no-shows.

Behind the screen
Scheduled rides are pooled at dispatch time, 20 minutes before pickup.
E17

Alerts

The rider is told at every step, newest first: cab on the way (with the OTP), cab at Gate 2, trip started, arrived at Blast Furnace, and the charge to CC-4471.

Behind the screen
Every message is logged with its delivery status. In the MVP they are in-app; SMS, WhatsApp and push plug in behind the same service.
E18

Me

The HR details behind every booking, the cost centre, whether exclusive rides need approval, the no-show count ("0 of 3 allowed"), and the privacy note.

Behind the screen
Three no-shows in 30 days pause booking (X3). Plant Ride tracks vehicles, not people.

Journey 2 of 3 · phone app mounted in the cab

The driver signs on and runs the queue

Often wearing gloves, often in sunlight: big targets and one job per screen. If drivers skip the app there is no live data, so simplicity here is a requirement.

Signed in asDRV-2281VehicleC-12 · MH 12 AB 4412 · LHS own fleet
D1

Start-of-duty check

The driver's licence and gate pass with their validity, then the vehicles this driver may take. C-12 is pre-selected and flags PUC certificate expires in 6 days. C-21 is on duty with A. Singh, and C-44 is off-road.

Behind the screen
An expired licence, gate pass, insurance, fitness or PUC blocks the duty outright (X4). Own-fleet drivers see own-fleet cabs; vendor drivers see their vendor's.

"Compliance is enforced here, not discovered at the gate."

D2

Vehicle check and odometer

Five checks: tyres, lights, seat belts, fuel, first-aid kit. The opening odometer is pre-filled from the last recorded reading (41,208 km).

Do
Tick all five, then Sign on for duty.
Behind the screen
The queue stays locked until the check is complete. A reading below the last recorded one is refused. This reading starts the day's distance log.
D3

The trip queue fills itself

Straight after sign-on: Route 1 · North corridor, 0 of 3 on board, speed limit 20 km/h. Stops in route order: Gate 2 (pick up 2), Coke Plant (drop 1), Blast Furnace (drop 1). One big Arrived at Gate 2 button, plus Navigate and SOS.

Behind the screen
M. Iyer and S. Banerjee were waiting at Gate 2 with no cab free. They were matched to C-12 the moment the duty started. When A. Raghavan books (E6), Gate 2 becomes "Pick up 3".

"The sequence comes from the route master, not the driver. No call from the desk."

D4

At Gate 2

After Arrived at Gate 2, each waiting rider gets a card: name, P. No., destination, Call, an OTP field with Board, and a No-show in 5:00 countdown.

Do
Type each rider's code and tap Board. Co-riders: M. Iyer 9317, S. Banerjee 5540.
Behind the screen
A no-show can only be marked after the route's 5-minute wait, and the cab cannot leave while a rider here is neither boarded nor marked.
D5

A wrong code

"That code does not match A. Raghavan. 4 tries left."

Behind the screen
Every wrong attempt is recorded, even though the request fails. After five, boarding for that rider locks until the desk verifies them, so the 4-digit code cannot be guessed.
D6

All aboard

3 of 3 on board, 0 seats left, status On trip. All three riders show OTP OK. Next: Arrived at Coke Plant (drop 1 rider, about 10 min).

Behind the screen
The rider's screen switches to Trip in progress (E11) and the desk board shows C-12 on trip, 3 of 3 (K4).
D7

First drop: Coke Plant

"M. Iyer dropped". 2 of 3 on board. Gate 2 is marked done. Next: Arrived at Blast Furnace, drop 2 riders.

Behind the screen
Arriving at a stop completes every ride that ends there. In the plant, a GPS ping inside the stop's geofence records the arrival without a tap.
D8

Trip closed

After the last drop: Waiting for pickups, 1 trip and 3 riders so far, and "No pickups yet. New riders appear here automatically, in route order."

Behind the screen
The last drop closes the trip: it is costed on route kilometres and split across the riders' cost centres. The cab is free, so anyone waiting is matched to it straight away.
D9

Report an issue

Breakdown, accident, delay, fuel entry or other, a short description, and Send to transport desk.

Behind the screen
A breakdown takes the cab off the road, puts its riders back in the queue from the last stop reached (same OTP), and alerts the desk. A delay notifies the riders on board.
D10

Close duty

The duty so far (vehicle, start time, hours used of 9, trips, riders, distance) and the close-out: closing odometer and fuel drawn.

Do
Enter the closing reading and fuel, tap Sign off duty.
Behind the screen
Sign-off is refused while a trip is still open.
D11

Duty closed

1 trip completed, 3.4 km GPS distance, 3 riders carried, and GPS km matches the odometer (odometer 6 km against 3.4 km on trips).

Behind the screen
This is where the vendor bill and the fuel log come from. An odometer reading that undercuts GPS, or runs far beyond it, is flagged for review.

Journey 3 of 3 · web console on the desk monitor

The transport desk runs the day and closes the month

The admin journey makes the other two work: every rule the rider and driver experience is set here.

Signed in asadminRoleFleet admin & desk (R. Kapoor)
K1

Live board

The whole fleet on one screen, refreshed every 5 seconds. Across the top: vehicles in service (3 of 9), seat occupancy today, average wait, on-time pickups, riders waiting and open alerts. Left: every vehicle with its state, route, riders and driver. Right, Needs attention: C-09's expired insurance (blocked from dispatch), K. Joshi's expired gate pass (blocked from duty), C-07's overspeed, C-44's breakdown.

Do
Acknowledge or Resolve an alert; Take off-road sends a cab's riders back to matching.

"An expired document blocks dispatch outright. It is not a warning."

K2

Queue

Before the driver signs on: P. Sharma's exclusive ride waits for approval (Approve / Reject), and M. Iyer and S. Banerjee wait at Gate 2. Under each waiting rider, the engine says why no cab could take them: C-21's driver is 8 h 52 into a 9-hour shift, C-07 and C-33 are on other routes, C-44 is off-road.

Behind the screen
Every decision is auditable. A request no cab can serve within 90 minutes is closed and the rider asked to book again.
K3

Assign a cab: the override

A fourth rider, N. Gupta, wants Gate 2 to Admin Block while C-12 is full (X1). Assign a cab lists every cab on duty with the rule that rules it out, down to "C-12 is at its 3-rider cap (3 of 3 between Gate 2 and Admin Block)".

Behind the screen
The desk can override a match, reassign a rider or book by hand, but the hard rules (occupancy cap, documents, duty hours) still apply to an override.
K4

The board during the trip

Vehicles in service is now 4 of 9. C-12 shows On trip on Route 1, Gate 2 to Blast Furnace, 3 of 3 riders, driver S. Kumar, with its amber document flag.

Behind the screen
One booking became a driver's pickup and a line on the desk board, without anyone making a call.
K5

Billing & cost centres

The month at a glance: trips, rides charged, GPS distance, transport cost and riders per trip. Charges by cost centre with budget bars that turn amber at 80% and red at 100%. Vendor bill vs GPS: Vendor A matches; Vendor B billed 21.2 km (6.5%) more than GPS recorded and is flagged as a dispute. Compliance watch summarises expiring and expired documents.

Do
Export to SAP (CSV): one line per ride for the month-end ERP import. ◀ ▶ switches month.

"GPS kilometres are the contractual source. That cross-check pays for itself in the first month."

K6

Routes & rules

Route 1's rules: max passengers per pick-up 3, max wait at a stop 5 min, max detour for riders on board 7 min, zone speed limit 20 km/h. Edit with − and +, then Save rules.

Behind the screen
The change applies to the next match and is written to the audit trail. Raising the cap lets a waiting rider in immediately. Two admins cannot overwrite each other: a stale save is refused.

"The three-rider cap is a setting per route. LHS changes it here, and no software release is needed."

K7

Stop sequence and document compliance

Route 1's stops in order (Gate 2, Admin Block, Coke Plant, Blast Furnace, Gate 2) with the minutes and kilometres of each leg and arrows to reorder them. Below, every document that is expiring or expired: K. Joshi's gate pass and C-09's insurance (blocked), C-12's PUC and C-33's insurance (6 days).

Behind the screen
Resequencing is refused while the route has rides booked or running, because those rides refer to positions in the old order. Alerts go out at 30, 7 and 1 days before expiry.
K8

Audit trail

Who booked, who changed it, who approved and who overrode, newest first: the day's sign-on, bookings, the completed trip with its kilometres and cost, and the sign-off.

Behind the screen
Every state-changing action writes one line, committed together with the action itself. Demo tools at the bottom reload the starting data between rehearsals.
K9

The desk on a tablet

The same board on a tablet at the gate, signed in as the desk role (desk, J. Thomas). Navigation moves to the bottom, and only Live board and Queue appear.

Behind the screen
Billing and routes & rules are admin-only, enforced by the server, not just hidden in the app.

Exception paths

When things go wrong

The happy path sells the product. These paths decide whether people keep using it.

X1

The cab is full

N. Gupta (LHS-44190) asks for Gate 2 to Admin Block while C-12 carries three. The shared option reads "Every cab is busy or full · you get the next free one".

Behind the screen
C-12 is dropped by the cap before scoring. Two riders racing for one last seat cannot both get it: the engine locks the route while it decides.
X2

Finding your cab

Confirmed anyway: "You are in the queue and get the next free cab automatically. The transport desk can see your request." Cancel ride stays available.

Behind the screen
Waiting requests are matched every 15 seconds and whenever a cab frees up. The desk sees them in the queue (K3).
X3

Booking paused after no-shows

H. Patel (LHS-52287) sees "Booking is paused: 3 no-shows in the last 30 days. Contact the transport desk to restore booking." Where to? is disabled.

Behind the screen
A no-show is recorded against policy, not argued at the gate. The count and window are settings LHS owns.
X4

Duty blocked

K. Joshi (DRV-2310): "Duty is blocked. K. Joshi's gate pass expired on 27 Sep 2026." The gate pass shows in red as expired 3 days ago.

Behind the screen
The same block applies to a cab: C-09's expired insurance keeps it out of every match and every sign-on.
