Overview

Scope at a glance

The MVP covers one site, the three configured routes and the full trip lifecycle: booking, matching, pooling within the occupancy cap, OTP boarding, drop, cost allocation and audit. It runs as one Expo app (Android, iOS and web) on a Spring Boot API with MySQL.

3user roles with their own app: user, driver, admin (plus a desk-only role)
27screens and screen states in scope, shown in this document
3routes, 9 vehicles, own and vendor fleet in the demo data
3riders per pick-up run at most (configurable per route)

Part 1 · Rider app

Users (employee) screens

The employee's app: book in under 30 seconds, see the cab and driver, share a tracking link, board with a 4-digit OTP, rate the ride and see what it cost. Four tabs: Home, Rides, Alerts, Me.

U1

Sign in

One sign-in screen for every role; the app opens the right side for the person.

In scope

  • Sign in with P. No. (users), driver ID (drivers) or username (desk)
  • One-tap demo accounts below the form
  • Session lasts 12 hours (one shift); server address shown for support

Rules

  • Each role lands in its own app and cannot open another role's screens.

Not in the MVP: SSO or Active Directory login; password reset flow.

U2

Home

The starting point for every ride.

In scope

  • Where to? button opens booking
  • Saved places with this month's trip counts; one tap starts a booking
  • Upcoming ride shortcut
  • The employee's cost centre, shown before booking

Rules

  • Every ride is charged to this cost centre on measured GPS distance.
  • Booking is disabled while the employee is paused for no-shows (U12).
U3

Book a ride

Choose where from, where to, when and how many riders on one screen.

In scope

  • From and To from a searchable stop list with zones
  • When: Now, In 30 min, In 1 hour, Tomorrow 08:00, Tomorrow 17:30
  • Seats (riders travelling)
  • Rider details prefilled from HR, editable; Booking for a guest

Rules

  • The route is resolved from the stop sequence.
  • Pick-up runs carry 3 riders at most; a larger group needs an exclusive cab.
  • A guest ride is charged to the host's cost centre.

Not in the MVP: a free date and time picker in the app (the API accepts any time up to 7 days ahead); recurring bookings.

U4

Choose your ride

Compare the ways to travel and confirm one.

In scope

  • Route summary: distance, time, pickup, seats
  • Shuttle: timetable information only, walk up
  • Shared cab: the cab, its ETA and seats taken
  • Exclusive cab: whole cab, needs approval
  • Cost centre, rider and contact shown before Confirm ride

Rules

  • A full cab is never offered; the rider gets the next cab and its ETA.
  • A repeated tap on Confirm returns the same booking, never a duplicate.
U5

Ride confirmed

Everything the rider needs to meet the cab, without phoning the desk.

In scope

  • Booking code and live status with a five-stage progress bar
  • Vehicle number, cab code, driver name and Call
  • Promised pickup time
  • 4-digit boarding OTP
  • Live tracking link with Share trip and Copy link
  • Cancel ride before boarding

Rules

  • The screen refreshes every 5 seconds.
  • An exclusive request shows Waiting for approval until the desk or HOD releases it.
U6

Shared tracking page

A public page anyone with the link can open, with no login.

In scope

  • Stage, route, vehicle number, cab code, last seen stop, stop list
  • Refreshes every 5 seconds
  • This trip has ended once the ride closes

Rules

  • Tracks vehicles, not people: no names or phone numbers are served.
  • The link expires when the trip closes.
  • The page cannot be embedded in another site.

Not in the MVP: a live map of the plant roads (stop progress is schematic).

U7

Cab arrived and trip in progress

The booking screen changes by itself as the trip moves on.

In scope

  • Your cab has arrived: OTP in large digits to show the driver, riders on this pick-up
  • Trip in progress: drop ETA, vehicle, driver, stops on this run, co-riders

Rules

  • The cab waits the route's wait time (5 min on Route 1); then the driver may mark a no-show.
  • Seats above the cap are held free by policy.
U8

Trip complete and rating

Close the ride: what it cost, who paid, and how it went.

In scope

  • Route, times, distance and ride type
  • Amount charged and the cost centre
  • 1–5 stars, quick tags (On time, Clean cab, Safe driving, Long wait, Hard to find) and a note
  • Booking record: code, rider, distance, cost centre

Rules

  • Trip cost = GPS route km × rate card, split across riders by distance ridden; shares add up to the paisa.
  • Ratings feed the vendor SLA report.
U9

My trips

Every ride the employee has taken or booked, for expense queries.

In scope

  • Upcoming, Past and Cancelled tabs
  • Each ride: date, route, distance, cab, cost, rating; tap for the full record

Rules

  • Ride history is kept for billing and safety only.

Not in the MVP: downloadable monthly statement for the employee.

U10

Alerts

Every step of a ride as a notification.

In scope

  • Cab assigned (vehicle, driver, phone, OTP), cab at the stop, trip started, arrived, charged
  • Scheduled ride confirmations; unread badge on the tab
  • Delivery status logged for every notification

Not in the MVP: push, SMS and WhatsApp delivery (in-app only; gateways plug in behind the notification service).

U11

Me

The employee's profile and the rules that apply to them.

In scope

  • P. No., email, contact, grade, cost centre
  • Whether exclusive rides need approval
  • No-shows in the last 30 days against the allowance
  • Privacy note and Sign out

Not in the MVP: editing the profile in the app (corrections go through HR); Hindi language option.

U12

Exception states

What the user sees when a ride cannot go ahead normally.

In scope

  • Every cab is busy or full: the rider waits in the queue and gets the next free cab automatically; the desk sees the request
  • Booking is paused: after 3 no-shows in 30 days; the rider contacts the desk
  • Waiting for approval: exclusive ride until released
  • Desk cancellation shows the desk's reason

Rules

  • The engine retries waiting requests every 15 seconds; unserved requests expire after 90 minutes.

Part 2 · Driver app

Driver screens

Built for use in the cab: large targets usable with gloves, pickups in route order, and a permanent SOS button while on duty. Three tabs: Trips, Issues, Duty.

D1

Sign in

The shared sign-in screen, with the driver ID.

In scope

  • Driver ID and password; 12-hour session
  • Lands on the start-of-duty check, or on the trip queue if already on duty
D2

Start-of-duty check

Compliance is checked here, before dispatch, not at the gate.

In scope

  • Driver's documents with validity: driving licence, gate pass
  • Vehicle choice; unavailable vehicles greyed out with the reason
  • Vehicle documents flagged ahead of expiry (e.g. PUC in 6 days)
  • Five-point check: tyres, lights, seat belts, fuel, first-aid kit and extinguisher
  • Opening odometer, prefilled from the last reading

Rules

  • An incomplete check is refused.
  • Expired licence, gate pass, insurance, fitness or PUC blocks duty (D8).
  • One driver per vehicle and one vehicle per driver at a time.
D3

Trip queue

The screen a driver lives in all day.

In scope

  • Header: vehicle, route, on board, seats left, speed limit, status
  • Next action as one large button, e.g. Arrived at Gate 2
  • Stops in route order with pickups, drops and ETAs
  • Navigate (opens map directions) and SOS
  • New riders appear automatically
  • Slow down banner on overspeed; Location is off banner

Rules

  • Riders already waiting are matched the moment the driver signs on.
  • The sequence comes from the route master, not the driver.
  • GPS pings are sent while on duty; overspeed alerts the desk.
D4

At the stop: OTP boarding and no-show

Board the right rider into the right cab.

In scope

  • One card per rider: name, P. No., destination, Call
  • OTP entry and Board
  • No-show countdown, then Mark no-show
  • Clear message on a wrong code with tries left; Boarding locked after too many

Rules

  • 4-digit OTP per booking; 5 wrong codes lock boarding for that rider.
  • The driver cannot leave a stop while a rider there is still waiting.
  • No-show allowed only after the route's wait time.
D5

On trip, drops and trip close

Drive the run and drop each rider in order.

In scope

  • Boarded riders listed as OTP OK; on board count updates
  • Arrived at … for each drop, with distance and time to it
  • After the last drop: Waiting for pickups with today's trips, riders and duty time

Rules

  • A GPS ping inside the stop's geofence records arrival automatically; the button works everywhere.
  • The last drop closes the trip, calculates the cost and expires tracking links.
D6

Issues and SOS

Tell the desk what went wrong, from the cab.

In scope

  • Breakdown, Accident, Delay, Fuel entry, Other, with details
  • Send to transport desk
  • SOS (Trips tab) sends cab and location to the desk and plant security, after a confirmation

Rules

  • A breakdown takes the cab off-road and puts its riders back in the queue from the last stop reached.
  • Every issue raises a desk alert.

Not in the MVP: photo attachments; offline buffering of issues and GPS.

D7

Duty and sign-off

End the shift with a distance check the vendor bill relies on.

In scope

  • Current duty: vehicle, start time, duty hours against the limit, trips, riders, distance, opening odometer
  • Close duty: closing odometer and fuel drawn
  • Duty closed summary with the odometer-vs-GPS check

Rules

  • Finish your trip first: sign-off is blocked with riders on board.
  • Duty limit 9 hours; the engine will not assign a trip that would break it.
  • GPS km is the contractual source for billing.
D8

Duty blocked

An expired document stops the driver before they reach a rider.

In scope

  • Duty is blocked with the document and its expiry date
  • Expired items shown in red on the document list

Rules

  • Expiry blocks duty and dispatch; it is not just a warning.
  • Alerts go to the desk at 30, 7 and 1 days before expiry and on expiry.

Part 3 · Web console

Admin screens

The transport desk's console, in a browser on a laptop or desk monitor (a tablet layout works at the gate). The admin sees four sections; a desk operator sees the first two.

A1

Live board

The whole fleet on one screen, refreshed every 5 seconds.

In scope

  • KPIs: vehicles in service, seat occupancy, average wait, on-time pickups, riders waiting, open alerts
  • Every vehicle: status, route, position, riders on board, driver
  • Take off-road / Back on road
  • Needs attention: document expiry, overspeed, breakdown, SOS, long waits; Acknowledge and Resolve
  • Waiting riders with a link to the queue

Rules

  • Acknowledging an alert does not unblock a vehicle with an expired document.
  • Each alert shows its severity, age and source.
A2

Queue and approvals

Every rider waiting for a cab or for approval, with the engine's reason.

In scope

  • Request cards: status, ride type, route, rider, P. No., cost centre, minutes waiting
  • The engine's note on why no cab was assigned
  • Exclusive rides: Approve or Reject with a reason
  • Cancel with a reason shown to the rider

Rules

  • An approved exclusive ride waits for an empty cab.
  • Every approval, rejection and cancellation is audited.
A3

Assign a cab (desk override)

The desk can place a rider on a specific cab, within the hard rules.

In scope

  • Every vehicle listed as feasible (ETA, seats taken, Assign) or refused with the reason
  • Cancel dialog with a reason field

Rules

  • Hard rules still apply to an override: occupancy cap, documents, duty hours.
  • A cab at its 3-rider cap is refused, e.g. C-12 is at its 3-rider cap.
A4

Billing and cost centres

Every kilometre on a cost centre, ready for the ERP. Admin only.

In scope

  • Month selector with Draft or Closed status
  • Totals: trips, rides charged, GPS km, cost, riders per trip
  • Charges by cost centre with budget bars
  • Vendor bill vs GPS, with gap and dispute flag
  • Compliance watch; Export to SAP (CSV)

Rules

  • Budget warning at 80%, over budget at 100%.
  • GPS km is the contractual source; a vendor claim above GPS is flagged.
  • One cost allocation per ride; no double billing.

Not in the MVP: direct posting to SAP (CSV export only); closing a month from the screen.

A5

Routes and rules

Configuration, not code: changes apply immediately and are audited. Admin only.

In scope

  • Route chips; route summary (type, stops, time, distance, frequency)
  • Rules per route: max passengers, max wait, max detour, zone speed limit; Undo and Save rules
  • Stop sequence with leg minutes and km; reorder and Save sequence
  • Document compliance list for drivers and vehicles

Rules

  • Saving rules asks for confirmation and shows each change, before and after.
  • Two admins cannot overwrite each other: a stale save is refused.
  • New trips follow a new sequence; vehicles never double back.
A6

Audit trail and demo tools

Who did what, when, for every state change.

In scope

  • Bookings, cancellations, approvals, duty start and end, trips, alerts, rule changes, with person and time
  • Reset demo data (demo builds only)

Rules

  • The audit trail is written by the server, not the screens.
  • The reset tool is switched off outside the demo.
A7

Roles: admin and desk operator

The same console, trimmed to the person's role.

In scope

  • Admin: Live board, Queue, Billing, Routes & rules
  • Desk operator: Live board and Queue

Rules

  • Enforced in the app and on the server.
